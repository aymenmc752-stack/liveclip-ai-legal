LiveClip AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Information We Use

LiveClip AI may process TikTok account information, authorization tokens, video files selected by users, captions, and publishing preferences.

How Information Is Used

This information is used to connect TikTok accounts and upload videos when users request those features.

Storage and Security

Video files, settings, and authorization tokens may be stored locally on the computer running the app. Users should keep their devices secure and protect their authorization credentials.

Third-Party Services

TikTok processes information according to its own privacy policy and terms when its services are used.

Your Choices

Users can stop using the app and revoke TikTok access through TikTok account settings. Locally stored data can be deleted from the computer running the app.
